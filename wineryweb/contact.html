FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The answers everyone asks for, before the bottles do.

How long does juice stay fresh?

Our cold-pressed blends are best within 72 hours of pressing. Each bottle carries a small "pressed" date on the cap — and we deliver within hours, not days.

Do you add sugar or water?

Never. Every ingredient on the label is real fruit (and occasional mint, ginger or lime). Sweetness comes from the fruit itself — nothing else goes in.

Can I pause my subscription?

Yes. Pause, swap blends or reschedule deliveries anytime from your subscription dashboard. No fees, no awkward phone calls.

Do you deliver outside the city?

We deliver same-morning across the metro area, and 48-hour refrigerated shipping nationwide for one-off orders and subscriptions.

Can cafés stock VINTAGE?

Absolutely. We supply over eighty cafés and markets with weekly wholesale drops, exclusive blends and branded chillers. See our Services page.

How do returns work?

If a bottle arrives less than perfect, we replace it within one morning — no return shipping, no forms. Send a photo and we'll handle the rest.
