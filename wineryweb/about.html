Our Story

VINTAGE began with a simple idea: real fruit, pressed the same morning it's picked, delivered before lunch. The same patience now fills our cellars — quiet vintages aged slowly, grown without shortcuts, bottled without compromise.

Who We Are

Pressed Fresh, Never Compromised

Founded in a single garage press-house, VINTAGE now works with forty family-run farms across the valley. Our pressing windows are short by design — fruit goes from tree to bottle in under twelve hours, through a slow cold-press that keeps vitamins, minerals and bright flavor exactly where they belong.

No concentrates. No added sugar. No shortcuts. Just whole fruit, a squeeze of care, and twelve blends pressed again fresh every single morning.

Our Journey

Milestones on the Road

2015

The Garage Press-House

VINTAGE presses its first one hundred bottles from a borrowed garage, selling out at the local farmers' market in two hours.

2017

Six-Fruit Formula

Our signature 6-in-1 blend debuts — six super-fruits in a single bottle — and quickly becomes the brand's heartbeat.

2020

Subscription Launch

Same-morning delivery subscriptions begin, bringing pressed-at-6am juice to hundreds of doorsteps by 10am.

2022

The Second Press House

A climate-friendly second facility opens, powered fully by solar, cutting delivery distances in half.

2026

Fresh Taste Award

VINTAGE takes home the Fresh Taste Award for the third consecutive year — for a juice that proves patience tastes best.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Farm First

Every blend begins at the farm. We pay our growers fairly, visit weekly, and press only what's ripe that morning.

No Added Sugar

Sugar is what the fruit already has. We press it slowly and cold, so sweetness arrives naturally — nothing else goes in.

Zero Waste

Pulp returns to the farms as compost and feed. Every bottle is recycled where possible, every press accounted for.
